Project 4 – Reading an Annual Report

Company

Safari Industries (India) Limited

Financial Year

FY 2025–26

Project Overview

This project focuses on analysing the annual report of Safari Industries (India) Limited to understand its business operations, financial performance, major business risks, management outlook and corporate governance.

Project Objectives

- Understand the company's business activities.
- Analyse financial performance for FY 2025–26 and FY 2024–25.
- Calculate financial growth percentages using Excel.
- Identify major business risks.
- Understand management's future plans.
- Examine corporate governance practices.

Project Sections

1. Business Overview
2. Chairman's Message – Report Note
3. Financial Performance Analysis
4. Major Business Risks
5. Management Outlook
6. Corporate Governance Analysis
7. Annual Report Reference
